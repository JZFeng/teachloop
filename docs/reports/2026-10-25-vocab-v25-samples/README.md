## 例句与近义词（每词前 2 条）

### struggle
- She struggled to open the jar.（她费力地打开罐子。）— *struggle to do something*
- He struggled with the heavy box.（他费力地搬着那个重箱子。）— *struggle with something*
- 近义：strive（更正式，强调为达成目标而付出巨大努力，不一定有阻力）；fight（强调对抗性的斗争，可能涉及武力）

### apple
- She ate an apple for breakfast.（她早餐吃了一个苹果。）— *eat an apple*
- The apple tree in the garden is full of fruit.（花园里的苹果树结满了果实。）— *apple tree*

### quixotic
- His quixotic plan to end world hunger with a single app was doomed to fail.（他想用一个应用解决世界饥饿问题的空想计划注定要失败。）— *quixotic plan*
- She had a quixotic belief that she could change the company from within.（她有一种不切实际的信念，认为自己能从内部改变公司。）— *quixotic belief*
- 近义：idealistic（比 quixotic 更中性，仅强调理想主义，不强调荒唐）；impractical（强调不实用，但缺少 quixotic 的理想主义色彩）

### serendipity
- Meeting her was pure serendipity.（遇见她纯属机缘巧合。）— *pure serendipity*
- The discovery was a happy serendipity.（这个发现是一个令人愉快的意外。）— *a happy serendipity*
- 近义：chance（泛指偶然性，不含'美好'意味）；luck（泛指好运，不强调'意外发现'）

### acquire
- She acquired a good knowledge of French while living in Paris.（她在巴黎生活期间学到了丰富的法语知识。）— *acquire a knowledge of something*
- He acquired the habit of reading before bed.（他养成了睡前阅读的习惯。）— *acquire the habit of doing something*
- 近义：obtain（更正式，强调通过努力得到具体东西）；gain（强调逐渐获得，常接经验、利益等抽象名词）

### ambiguous
- The wording of the contract is ambiguous.（合同的措辞模棱两可。）— *the wording is ambiguous*
- His answer was deliberately ambiguous.（他的回答故意含糊其辞。）— *deliberately ambiguous*
- 近义：vague（更强调不具体、模糊，不一定有多种解释）；equivocal（更正式，常指故意含糊以误导）

### hierarchy
- She quickly rose through the corporate hierarchy.（她迅速在公司等级体系中晋升。）— *rise through the hierarchy*
- The church has a strict hierarchy of authority.（教会有严格的权力等级制度。）— *a hierarchy of authority*
- 近义：ranking（更强调排列顺序，不一定有权力上下级）；pecking order（更口语化，常指群体中的实际地位高低）

### trivial
- Don't waste time on trivial details.（不要把时间浪费在琐碎的细节上。）— *trivial details*
- It was a trivial mistake, easily fixed.（那是个小错误，很容易修正。）— *a trivial mistake*
- 近义：insignificant（更正式，强调无足轻重）；unimportant（更通用，直接表示不重要）

### bolster
- The new evidence bolsters the case for reform.（新证据支持了改革的理由。）— *bolster the case for something*
- We need to bolster her confidence before the exam.（考试前我们需要增强她的信心。）— *bolster someone's confidence*
- 近义：strengthen（更通用，强调使更强壮或更牢固，不特指提供额外支持）；reinforce（强调通过增加材料或力量来加固，比 bolster 更具体）

### refute
- She refuted the claim that the vaccine was unsafe.（她驳斥了疫苗不安全的说法。）— *refute the claim that*
- The scientist refuted the theory with new data.（科学家用新数据驳斥了该理论。）— *refute something with something*
- 近义：disprove（强调用证据证明错误，比 refute 更正式）；rebut（正式，特指对指控或论点的逐一反驳）
