Overall Assignment Rubric

Essential Question Peer Review

Scoring Rubric

1. Required Length — 2 points
If the learner uploaded a video, was it between 1–4 minutes?
If the learner uploaded a document or typed the response in the box, was it between 1–4 paragraphs?
- Yes: 2 points
- No: 0 points

2. Clear Position — 4 points
Did the learner clearly express an opinion about the statement:
“Anyone who speaks the language can teach the language”?
- Yes: 4 points
- No: 0 points

3. Explanation and Supporting Details — 4 points
Did the learner explain their opinion and incorporate relevant details supporting the opinion?
- Yes: 4 points
- No: 0 points

Maximum Score: 10 points

Optional Feedback:
Provide brief, constructive feedback that is useful to the learner.
