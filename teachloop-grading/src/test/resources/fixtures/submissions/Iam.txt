Iam Aya, and I am from Sudan. My first language is Arabic. Generally,I disagree with the statement "anyone who speaks the language can teach the language''. I write and speak my language perfectly, does that mean I can teach it? Of course not!Why? Because the language has difficult rules to handle.

For example, when you write a sentence in Arabic, you have to know if it is a nominal sentence or a verbal one so you write it correctly. Also, if you want to speak it, there are phonemes only found in this language. That does not mean it is a hard language, but you have to study it the right way with a professional. I am a native speaker, but it is hard for me to explain it.
