Hello, my name is Regan and I disagree with the Essential Assumption. Not everyone who speaks the language can teach the language. Linguistics is complex and not everyone has the depth of knowledge or the skills to effectively teach others a new language.

Teachers must have a solid understanding of the material they are teaching in order to make sure students are learning the correct things and to answer any questions that may come up. As mentioned in this module many different skills are necessary to properly engage students. A teacher needs to communicate in certain ways, such as by speaking slowly and clearly and by considering their use of body language. They also need to use strategies such as scaffolding to build on topics for students to effectively absorb new concepts and develop new abilities. Not all people know enough about the sort of things needed in order to teach others.
