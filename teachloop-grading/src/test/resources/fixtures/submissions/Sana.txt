My name is Sana. I have been studying English for a long time, and initially, I thought that as soon as I started speaking the language, I would automatically be able to teach it. I believed that having language content was the only requirement to become a great teacher.
However, reality taught me otherwise. When I started teaching, I used to be a lecturer-style teacher who spoke most of the time and overwhelmed my students with what I now recognize as "i+10" instead of comprehensible input (i+1). I realized that knowing the language is completely different from knowing how to teach it effectively.
Therefore, I strongly disagree with the statement "Anyone who speaks the language can teach the language." Speaking a language gives you the content, but to be a successful teacher, you must also master teaching techniques and methodology. Don't make the same mistake I did—focus on learning the right techniques alongside your content!
