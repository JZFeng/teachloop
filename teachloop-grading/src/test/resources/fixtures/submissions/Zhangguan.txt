I disagree with the statement, anyone who speaks the language can teach the language.

I agree with the idea that language is a cake. Just like the cake, the language itself is sweet and delicious, it is decorated and visually appealing, and made with a recipe from different ingredients.However, in order to teach a language, the teacher not only need to know how to make good cakes, but also how to package or deliver the cake to the customers.

Anyone who speaks the language would be able to tell what type of cake it is (which language), what the cake is made of (grammar, vocabulary), but they have to master the cake making skills, and the presentation/delivery skills before they can actually teach the language. Like you can not force your customer to buy a cake, teaching is a two-way experience, if the students don't 'buy', you can not call it 'teaching'.
