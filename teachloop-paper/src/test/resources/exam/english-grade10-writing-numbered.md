# 第四部分 写作示范参考答案

15. 应用文写作示范范文：

Dear Chen Ming,
I'm sorry to hear that you had a fight with your parents over phone use. It is completely normal to feel confused, but please remember that your parents are just concerned about your transition to high school.

To solve this problem, I suggest having an open talk with them. Explain calmly that you need your phone for communication and learning. Meanwhile, you can set boundaries together, such as limiting your screen time after study hours. Showing them your self-control will surely rebuild their trust.

Hope things get better soon!

Yours,  
Li Hua

16. 口语表达招新演讲示范范文：
